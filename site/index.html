Q5. Should the forecast have known? How much shortfall coincides with outages already published in REMIT at the period start?

Rule (recorded 12 October 2026, before any Q5 value was read): the share of positive unexplained shortfall in half-hours when an outage of the unit is already published in REMIT at the half-hour's start, split planned, unplanned, both and none; a measured share, with no verdict. The rule

Result: of 16,409,747.7 MWh of positive unexplained shortfall, 12.0% falls in half-hours with a published planned outage, 11.2% unplanned, 0.4% both and 76.4% none. By fuel group, the share with no known outage runs from 7.3% (nuclear) to 100.0% (coal, interconnector); wind 90.4%. "Known" means published in REMIT, not that the generator knew: an association, not a cause.

Q23. How close to their PNs do units run when not instructed?

Rule (registered 12 October 2026; interconnectors excluded the same day, before any read): per cohort unit and month, over half-hours with no acceptance in force, the absolute gap between PN and metered energy as a percentage of registry capacity; the median and 90th percentile per fuel group and month. A proxy for NESO's PN-accuracy measure: ABSVD and dynamic MEL/MIL are not used, so no unit is called inaccurate. The rule

Result: in September 2026 the median absolute percentage is 8.722 for wind (90th percentile 13.744), 2.300 for nuclear, 1.993 for other and 0.395 for gas. Wind's median is 7.795 on days with an SO-flagged wind bid and 4.532 on days without (363 and 42 days): an association, not a cause.

Months August 2025 to September 2026, from 22 August 2025; cutoff 30 September 2026 22:30 UTC. Units and half-hours for every point are in the saved output. Limitations

  • Fuel groups are the current registry's.
  • The metered value is the latest settlement run, which differs by date.
  • PN is the latest capture, not the gate-closure position.
  • Balancing services settled outside the balancing mechanism are not in BOALF.
  • The cohort is not the grid.
